## Verdict

- **REQUEST CHANGES**

Primary blockers:

3. Minor ADR drift (ADR-6 stdout.log vs chat.log; ADR-4 @dataclass for TestCase).

Once these are addressed, the implementation is otherwise solid: tests pass, ruff is clean, traceability is complete, atomic writes are correct, resume semantics are honored, and the `Experiment`/`Report` envelopes are now first-class and persisted.
